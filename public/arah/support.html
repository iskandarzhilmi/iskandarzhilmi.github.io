Arah Support

Arah can't find my location

Go to Settings, then Arah, then Location, and choose "While Using the App". Make sure Precise Location is on.

A route or search isn't working

Arah covers Malaysia, Singapore and Brunei. Check that you have mobile data and try again.

A report is wrong

When you pass a report, Arah asks "Still there?". Tap "Gone" and the report disappears sooner.
